Manifesto

The China Studies Society is more than a society noticeboard. It's where students go beyond stereotypes to understand China's history, culture, politics, economy and global influence — through genuine student perspectives.

  • 01We move beyond stereotypes and simplistic narratives.
  • 02We explore China's history, culture, politics and economy.
  • 03We host talks, panel discussions and cultural gatherings.
  • 04We connect students with academics, diplomats and industry experts.
  • 05We make King's part of a global conversation on China.
