More than a society noticeboard

It's where students go beyond stereotypes to understand China's history, culture, politics, economy and global influence, through genuine student perspectives.

  • We move beyond stereotypes and simplistic narratives.
  • We explore China's history, culture, politics and economy.
  • We host talks, panel discussions and cultural gatherings.
  • We connect students with academics, diplomats and industry experts.
  • We make King's part of a global conversation on China.

We create spaces to learn, explore, discuss, meet people, and share perspectives on China — whether you specialise in China studies or are simply curious about its role in our globalised world.
