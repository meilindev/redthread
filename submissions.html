Before you send

Who can submit
Students and external contributors. If you are not a KCL student, please say so in your email.
Length
800–2,500 words. We can discuss exceptions.
Format
Word document or Google Doc, sent as an attachment or link.
Language
English.
Deadline
Submissions are open on a rolling basis.
